**Think about it:**

- When was the last time someone congratulated you on something? How did it make you feel? *(Kapan terakhir kali ada yang memberi kamu ucapan selamat? Bagaimana perasaanmu?)*
- Do you say "congratulations" the same way to your teacher and to your best friend? *(Apakah kamu mengucapkan selamat dengan cara yang sama ke guru dan ke sahabatmu?)*
- What's the difference between a sincere compliment and just being polite? *(Apa bedanya pujian yang tulus dengan sekadar basa-basi sopan?)*
