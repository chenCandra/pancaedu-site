    body: "The effect of social media on teenagers has become a controversial topic among parents, teachers, and students themselves. Some people believe it brings many benefits, while others worry about its negative effects. On one hand, there are several arguments in favor of social media. Firstly, it helps teenagers stay connected with friends and family, even when they live far apart. Secondly, social media provides access to useful information, educational content, and creative communities where teenagers can learn new skills, such as photography, writing, or coding. It can also give quiet students a platform to express their opinions more comfortably. On the other hand, there are also strong arguments against social media use among teenagers. Firstly, spending too much time on social media can negatively affect a teenager's mental well-being, including increased anxiety and poor sleep. Secondly, teenagers may compare their lives unfairly to the curated, filtered lives they see online, which can lower their self-confidence. In addition, social media can expose teenagers to cyberbullying or false information that spreads quickly. Considering both sides, it can be concluded that social media itself is not entirely good or bad -- it depends on how it is used. Therefore, teenagers should be encouraged to use social media in moderation, balance their online time with offline activities, and think critically about what they see and share."
