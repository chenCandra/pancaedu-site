    body: "Rani and Dinda had been best friends since elementary school. They did everything together, from studying for exams to sharing their favorite snacks at recess. Everything changed, however, when Rani got her first smartphone. At first, it was exciting -- she could watch videos, chat with new online friends, and post photos every day. Slowly, Rani started spending more time scrolling through her phone than talking to Dinda in person. One afternoon, Dinda waited for Rani at their usual spot, but Rani didn't even notice her because she was busy replying to comments on her latest post. The biggest problem happened on Dinda's birthday. Rani completely forgot about it because she was distracted by a trending video. Dinda felt hurt and ignored. 'You always have time for your phone, but never for me,' Dinda said quietly the next day. Rani felt terrible. She realized that she had been choosing her screen over her real friend without even noticing. That evening, Rani visited Dinda's house with a small cake and said, 'I'm so sorry. You matter more to me than any app.' Dinda smiled, though she was still a little hurt. They talked for hours, just like before, with their phones left untouched on the table. From that day on, they agreed to have at least one 'phone-free hour' together every week, just to reconnect and truly listen to each other."
