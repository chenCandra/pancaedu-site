modelTexts:
  - title: "How to Make a Simple Water Filter"
    body: "Goal: To make a simple water filter that can remove dirt and small particles from cloudy water, using materials you can easily find at home. Materials: an empty plastic bottle, scissors, a piece of cloth or cotton, gravel, sand, and activated charcoal. Steps: First, ask an adult to help you cut the bottom off the plastic bottle, then turn the bottle upside down so the neck points downward. Next, place a small piece of cloth or cotton into the neck of the bottle to stop the other materials from falling through. After that, pour in a layer of charcoal, followed by a layer of sand, and finally a layer of gravel on top. Then, slowly pour the dirty water into the top of the filter and let it drip through the layers into a clean container below. Finally, check the water that comes out -- it should look clearer than before. Remember: this filter only removes dirt and particles, so you should still boil the filtered water before drinking it."
