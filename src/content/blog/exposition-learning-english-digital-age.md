Although Indonesian is our national language, English has become the main language of the internet, international communication, and global opportunities. Firstly, English is the language most commonly used for global communication online. Most websites, scientific journals, and international forums use English as their primary language. Without English skills, students miss out on a huge amount of information and knowledge that is simply not available in Indonesian. Secondly, English skills give students access to better education and career opportunities. Many scholarships, online courses, and international exchange programs require a good level of English. Moreover, companies increasingly look for employees who can communicate in English, especially in fields like technology, tourism, and business. Students who master English are therefore more competitive when applying for universities or jobs, both in Indonesia and abroad. Finally, English allows young people to connect with people and cultures from around the world through social media, games, and online communities, which can broaden their perspectives and build valuable skills in communication and critical thinking. For all these reasons, students should be encouraged to learn English seriously, not just to pass exams, but to open doors to a wider world of knowledge and opportunity."
